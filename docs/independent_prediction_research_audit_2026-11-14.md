Report date: **14 November 2026**. Repository: `C:\Workspace\Americas Cup\2D_regatta_replay`. Revision: `5dbb21597de980b14d851d4348c8c4d1f8d5bd16` (13 November). This was a read-only research and implementation audit; this report is the sole deliverable added. Literature was checked against primary papers and official sources available by the report date. Repository observations, published findings, and recommendations are distinguished below.

## 1. Executive assessment

**Build a verified, issuance-time race-results dataset and a common chronological evaluation before adding architecture.** Use a campaign-specific, partially pooled Bradley–Terry model for matches and Plackett–Luce for fleet rankings as the operational reference. Challenge it with a small model of strictly prior performance summaries. Keep the simulator as conditional research, and test one targeted improvement to its performance surface rather than expanding the entire physical system.

Confidence is **high in this ordering of investments**, but **low that any particular algorithm is already the best predictor**. Modern pretrained tabular models deserve a controlled comparison; small local sample size alone does not disqualify them. Neither recent benchmark victories nor the existing simulator establish credible AC38 winner probabilities.

The principal constraints are different:

- **Insufficient evidence:** few independent teams, campaigns and venues; incomplete independently supported labels; no demonstrated race-probability calibration.
- **Evaluation and integration defects:** inconsistent outcome ledgers, retrospective finisher fields, result-time cutoffs, exposed historical test data, and stale artifacts.
- **Modeling assumptions:** confounded empirical team effects, sampled future routes, weakly identified transfer, and incomplete propagation of fitted-model uncertainty.

The project is useful research infrastructure. Its current numerical outputs should retain their experimental or conditional status.

### Evaluation limitations that affect decisions

1. **Target/issuance mismatch:** AC75 component fitting predicts mean SOG using mean orientation from the same five-second window. This estimates conditional performance, not the next five seconds from information at window start. Race inference replaces observed orientation with sampled route templates, adding an unvalidated behavioral assumption. [Feature target](<C:/Workspace/Americas Cup/2D_regatta_replay/pipeline/kinematic_ml/features.py:623>)
2. **Retrospective field selection:** the AC38 AC40 backtest predicts among `target["order"]`, excluding entrants absent from the eventual order. Its loader mixes official and reconstructed orders without the production eligibility gate. Reported AC40 improvement, 1.590 versus 1.788 log loss, therefore does not establish full-roster pre-race skill. [Backtest](<C:/Workspace/Americas Cup/2D_regatta_replay/pipeline/prediction/ac38_backtest.py:138>)
3. **Wrong clock for operational claims:** this backtest cuts prior features/results at target result availability, rather than explicit pre-start issuance. Strictly earlier timestamps and fold-fitted standardization are implemented, but cannot alone prevent information arriving between start and publication. Actual race-start checks are needed; this audit does not assert every fold necessarily leaked. AC75 chronological result fitting does use earlier days. Team holdouts assess unseen-team transfer within AC37, not future-campaign prediction.
4. **Confounded effects and uncertainty:** empirical `team_residuals` are raw team-average SOG minus the pooled mean, shrunken by window count, rather than wind/angle-adjusted residuals. Conditions can masquerade as team strength. Runtime samples one fitted model plus events/routes/residuals; default text about three-seed calibration overstates propagated parameter uncertainty. [Offsets](<C:/Workspace/Americas Cup/2D_regatta_replay/pipeline/kinematic_ml/baselines.py:108>), [uncertainty text](<C:/Workspace/Americas Cup/2D_regatta_replay/pipeline/kinematic_ml/uncertainty.py:24>)
5. **Final-test exposure:** an earlier candidate used 2,578 reserved windows. Current exclusions are valuable but cannot restore untouched status. Registration files are replaceable, and the final-target access guard has no production callers. Preserve existing reservations and obtain a genuinely new confirmatory block. [Recorded exposure](<C:/Workspace/Americas Cup/2D_regatta_replay/experiments/repair_experiment_v1.json:25>)

The AC38 artifact also retains 22.459% unresolved tournament trials; its nested posterior endpoint refinement shifted 8.07 percentage points at the computation cap. Small ordinary Monte Carlo error does not establish stable posterior intervals, resolved rules, discrimination or calibration. Published inventory still says 130 AC75 source windows where current code/inventory count 115; this is stale artifact drift, not a remaining double-count in current code.

## 3. Research comparison and environmental complexity

### Relevant methods

| Method; publication status | Demonstrated evidence and applicability here |
|---|---|
| **Hierarchical BT/PL; dynamic ratings.** [Turner et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00180-020-00959-3), [Bayesian bpcs, 2021](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-021-01714-2), and [Glickman, 1999](https://www.glicko.net/research/glicko.pdf), peer-reviewed. | PL handles coherent rankings/partial orders; Bayesian BT supports hierarchical uncertainty; dynamic ratings represent evolving strength, with simulation/chess/tennis evidence. These are methodological foundations, not sailing victories. Partial pooling is suitable for sparse results; unconstrained drift or team-by-weather effects are weakly identified. Open CPU implementations, low maintenance. Tactical interference and campaign changes challenge their assumptions. |
| **Regularized tabular models and boosting.** [TabArena, NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/1697e3fb412da11dc9488249f9e7bbc9-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html), peer-reviewed. | Benchmark comparisons show strong trees, competitive foundation models on smaller tables, and substantial effects from tuning/ensembling. They also expose validation overfitting. Test ridge/logistic prior-summary effects and one shallow ensemble on identical causal rows. CPU costs are modest; campaign leakage and dependent observations remain the project's responsibility. |
| **Physics-guided sailing surface, SPAM.** [Olsson & Marimon Giovannetti, July 2026](https://doi.org/10.5957/jst/2026.11.1.238), peer-reviewed. | Authors report 15–18% lower conditional-speed error than RF/GAM on held-out regattas: 21 Nacra 17 crews, nine regattas, 28 physical basis parameters plus ridge/noise modeling. This is the closest targeted lead, not winner evidence. Boat physics and broader regatta diversity limit transfer. A small CPU reimplementation is feasible; no open implementation was located, and acquiring comparable wind is a dependency. |
| **Empirical polar / physical VPP.** [ORC 2026 technical documentation](https://orc.org/uploads/files/Rules-Regulations/2026/ORC-VPP-Documentation-2026.pdf), official, not an accuracy trial. | Force-balance methods require hull/appendage/sail measurements absent here. Fit a ground-relative empirical polar or regularized residual surface instead. A physical reference helps shape extrapolation but does not identify unobserved AC75 design/control effects. ORC's executable is subscription software; it is not a public AC75 digital twin. |
| **Pretrained tabular learning.** [TabPFN, January 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08328-6), peer-reviewed Nature; [TabICLv2, February/September 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11139v2), ICML 2026. | TabPFN demonstrated strong small-table classification/regression against tuned alternatives. TabICLv2 reports gains over RealTabPFN-2.5 on TabArena/TALENT; [public inference, pretraining and generator code](https://github.com/soda-inria/tabicl) make it a defensible open challenger. Synthetic pretraining diversity contributes information absent from locally trained networks. Benchmark priors may fail on connected competitors and campaign drift. Pin one default configuration; public weights/PyTorch suffice, GPU useful. |
| **Latest TabPFN-3.5.** [September report, revised 3 November 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17895v3#S2.SS2), technical report. | Aggregate benchmark gains are real reported comparisons, but tuned/ensembled MLPs still lead full temporal/grouped/large slices; small slices have uncertainty. Headline results mix public and proprietary Thinking variants. Weight licensing differs from Apache code. It merits consideration, not automatic preference over reproducible open candidates. |
| **Joint trajectory forecasting.** [QCNet, CVPR 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhou_Query-Centric_Trajectory_Prediction_CVPR_2023_paper.html); [MotionLM, ICCV 2023](https://waymo.com/research/motionlm/), peer-reviewed. | Leading Argoverse/Waymo driving benchmark results support joint future-motion representations and dependence modeling. They use extensive driving scenes/maps, not a handful of regattas. Driving weights do not supply sailing tactics. GPU training and maintenance are substantial; first beat constant-velocity/turn-rate and polar baselines on unseen sailing days. |

Across methods, extra data or a different target must not be credited as an algorithmic improvement. Transfer requires independent domains: [Wang et al., AAAI 2024](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i14.29497) demonstrate why many observations within few domains can still generalize poorly. Six paired AC37 team identities in one campaign cannot validate a stable AC37-to-AC38 transfer law.

Use chronological evaluation and proper scores. [Leave-future-out research](https://doi.org/10.1080/00949655.2020.1783262) explains future-information optimism; [CORP reliability diagnostics](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2016191118) improve calibration assessment without creating more independent data. [Conformal beyond exchangeability](https://doi.org/10.1214/23-AOS2276), peer-reviewed 2023, offers assumption-dependent robustness, not automatic AC38 coverage from correlated AC37 frames.

### Environmental variables: individual decisions

The 28 actual public environmental exports contain hourly air temperature, water temperature and wave height, labeled historical model/replay context. New provider definitions for currents, wave period/direction and forecast products do not establish acquired inputs or deployed features. Incremental predictive value has not been demonstrated for any of these additions.

| Variable | Physical role, evidence limitations and recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Currents** | Affect ground velocity, VMG and maneuver geometry. GPS SOG already includes advection: mechanically adding current to a SOG-trained polar double-counts it. No deployed acquired current field was found. Regional modeled surface vectors may miss coastal gradients and relevant depth. **Limited experiment** only with vector decomposition and matched space/time; defer production until day-held-out benefit is shown. |
| **Wave height** | Can affect foil stability, resistance and maneuvers. Existing hourly model estimates describe context, not boat-local wave encounters. Shared roughness may change all speeds without changing ranking. **Retain descriptive context; limited pooled speed-residual experiment**, with no team-specific wave response absent repeated independent exposure. |
| **Wave period and direction** | Encounter frequency and angle can matter beyond height. No actual exported acquisition was established. Require swell/wind-wave separation, “from/to” convention, propagation direction and averaging definitions. **Defer acquisition/model complexity** until height and baseline residuals show a reproducible need. |
| **Air temperature** | Affects air density and sail force; also correlates with date/venue and wind regimes. Existing retrospective hourly estimates provide little independent campaign variation. **Descriptive context**, not an initial production feature. Test only a pooled physical correction after controlling for wind and campaign. |
| **Water temperature** | Can affect fluid properties and equipment response, but recorded variation is entangled with venue/season and surface-model quality. **Descriptive context; defer predictive fitting** unless a physical mechanism and independent residual evidence emerge. |

Finer resolution is useful only when it improves measured forecast information. Copernicus [Mediterranean reanalysis](https://data.marine.copernicus.eu/product/MEDSEA_MULTIYEAR_PHY_006_004/description) is an assimilated regional model, not measured race current; [Open-Meteo marine documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/marine-weather-api) notes coastal limitations. Depth, vector conventions and averaging windows must be explicit. For operational forecasts preserve initialization, publication, acquisition and valid times: Open-Meteo's [historical forecast product](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api) stitches early run hours, while individual-run archives serve a different need. Retrospective fields and user scenarios support conditional analysis, not pre-race skill claims.

## 5. A minimal decisive experiment — proposed, not run

**Question:** do prior performance summaries add useful pre-race AC75 winner information beyond earlier results?

1. **Target/cutoff:** scored match winner, issued 15 minutes before the scheduled gun, with a documented update rule for delays. Use the registered two-boat field. Resolve DNS, stopped races, resails and jury corrections explicitly; a resail remains in its family. Official margin is secondary only where independently timed.
2. **Eligibility:** independently reviewed winner, campaign identity, family/day, actual start and source-availability evidence. Reconcile the 82-result source with the learned ledger first. Only results/summaries available before issuance enter; use previous-day summaries initially. Practice may train conditional motion only, with a declared domain indicator. Missing wind forecasts mean no realized pre-race weather feature.
3. **Frozen competitors:** equal chances, fixed Elo, regularized BT; one BT-plus-ridge model with prior upwind/downwind VMG and tack/gybe summaries and availability masks; one pinned default TabICLv2 on the same summaries. No broad tuning. The current simulator participates only after matching the same target, field and issuance eligibility.
4. **Splits:** expanding chronological whole-day folds within AC37; all preprocessing, feature aggregation, tuning and any calibration inside past training folds. Keep all attempts/checkpoints of a family together. Add series-level and leave-one-day sensitivity; repeated teams are allowed because within-campaign deployment repeats teams.
5. **Scores/uncertainty:** primary day-equal paired log-loss difference, secondary binary Brier. Report race-equal results, all eligibility exclusions and unrounded paired predictions. Use whole-day block intervals, with series-block sensitivity; 29 days and shared teams limit precision. Reliability and slope are descriptive until sufficient independent out-of-fold evidence exists.
6. **Decision:** predeclare an engineering-relevant gain, provisionally 0.02 nats per match, and a Brier noninferiority tolerance of 0.005. These are proposed decision thresholds, not measured results. Adopt added summaries only if the paired uncertainty interval supports the gain and findings survive grouping sensitivities. Otherwise retain the reference or conclude insufficient evidence. Keep identity calibration unless a training-only calibration experiment earns its place.
7. **Final test:** preserve current reservations and mark their exposure honestly. Freeze code, input hashes and selection before a genuinely new chronological regatta block. Determine its size using development-only power/sensitivity calculations; do not inspect its targets for feature choice. No historical rearrangement can create an untouched AC38 test. A within-AC37 success still requires prospective target-campaign evaluation.

This experiment can conclude that telemetry adds no useful value. Conditional speed research remains separately useful: compare one regularized polar correction against the installed empirical surface on unseen days, then test its effect on live predictions. Race-level adoption still requires improved race scores. Avoid changing routes, environmental fields and network architecture simultaneously.

## 6. Work to retain, defer or discontinue

**Retain:** source/event reconstruction, stream-quality filters, campaign identities, immutable artifacts, family exclusions, explicit unavailable states, fitted component integration, shared residual sampling and conservative tournament bounds.

**Prioritize:** independently reviewed outcome scope and nonfinish denominators; a shared issuance-time ledger; reconciled artifacts; genuinely new race-days; production/evaluation parity; and jointly condition-adjusted performance effects. These investments benefit every candidate.

**Defer:** full hydrodynamics, wave-period/direction response surfaces, flexible team/environment interactions, elaborate temporal drift, large trajectory networks and learned cross-campaign transfer. Acquire timestamped spatial wind or current only for a specific residual hypothesis with usable prediction-time coverage.

**Discontinue as evidence claims:** treating tests as accuracy, frames as independent races, conditional SOG fit as unseen-race skill, seed/Monte Carlo spread as total uncertainty, or repeatedly reused holdouts as final tests. Maintain the simulator as an experiment without investing in cosmetic numerical precision ahead of validation.

## 7. What would change the conclusion?

The recommendation would change with independently verified full fields and timings; archived issued-before-start weather; additional regattas spanning overlapping conditions and crews; prospective AC75/AC38 predictions; reproducible gains over results-only and persistence baselines; and intervals reliable across independent groups. A modern model winning that comparison should be adopted. A targeted hybrid that improves speed but not winner scores should remain a conditional-performance tool.

**Given the actual data, build the verified common race dataset and chronological benchmark next, then test prior summaries and one targeted within-class hybrid. Stop expanding environmental and simulator architecture until it earns a measurable improvement. The strongest credible predictions will come from better independent evidence and a matched evaluation, with model complexity decided by that evidence.**
