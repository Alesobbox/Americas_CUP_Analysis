# Narration for oral rehearsal

This is a reading script, not an audio file. Pause after each paragraph and explain its claim without looking. Numerical values describe the review snapshot of 4 November 2026.

The project begins with a practical question: what can we learn from recorded America's Cup training and race telemetry? It helps us watch past sessions, compare supported performance and inspect conditional forecasts. Those are three distinct activities. A replay can look convincing while a historical quantity remains unavailable, and a future forecast can be numerically valid while its predictive value remains unestablished.

The archive contains 28 source sessions: nine from Cagliari and 19 from Naples. There are 224 replayed boat-races and 227 scheduled entries. Every replayed boat is AC40. These counts describe different units. A boat-race is one boat's participation in one race. Thousands of timestamps from that boat do not make thousands of independent race outcomes. Common wind, course and interactions connect boats and repeated frames within each session.

Imagine the racecourse. A gate is a line between two marks. Boats start across a designated line, sail legs toward alternating gates and change direction by tacking upwind or gybing downwind. Speed describes how fast they move. Velocity also describes direction. Velocity made good is a projection of velocity toward a chosen direction. Wind-relative VMG and course-axis VMG use different directions and can disagree. Heading describes the boat's orientation, while course over ground describes actual movement over the ground. Do not substitute one for the other casually.

The raw source names assets, not simply team labels that can be assumed constant across files. The ingestion pipeline resolves identities within each session. It converts latitude and longitude to a local east-and-north coordinate system in metres. Source timestamps can be irregular and channels can have different cadence or gaps. The replay uses a regular 100 millisecond grid to coordinate the picture, but that grid is a reconstruction timeline, not a new sensor.

Position interpolation bridges source points. Angle interpolation must follow a wrapped angular difference: halfway from 350 degrees to 10 degrees is 0 degrees, not 180. A straight interpolated path through a missing interval may appear to cross a gate. That picture alone does not prove the exact event occurred. Marks can move, endpoint tolerances matter and source timing can conflict. Reconstruction calculates useful events under explicit approximations; it does not create an official racing ruling.

The Python pipeline writes replay arrays and historical artifacts. Historical analysis owns the formulas, units, windows, source support and missing-value rules. The browser consumes those exported values. It also performs instantaneous geometry for playback and display. Keeping those responsibilities separate prevents the same label from being defined two different ways in Python and TypeScript.

Every historical number needs its question. A mean speed asks about eligible observed speed over a defined window. A checkpoint gap asks about a shared passage location and timing. A continuous gap estimates current separation under geometry and speed assumptions. A maneuver retention quantity describes selected detected events with supported windows. A boundary quantity describes geometric exposure under modeled course polygons. None is automatically a causal statement about tactics or crew ability.

The peer performance indexes are especially easy to misread. For each matching completed eligible leg, compare the selected boat with the equal-boat mean of other eligible peers. Divide selected value by that peer mean and multiply by 100. Then average the leg indexes equally. The selected boat is excluded from its own baseline. Different boats and different legs can have different eligible peer sets.

Here is a synthetic example. The selected boat sails 20 and 30 knots over two eligible legs. The corresponding peer means are 10 and 30. The leg indexes are 200 and 100, averaging 150. But the selected raw mean is 25 and the peer raw mean is 20, whose ratio times 100 is 125. Both arithmetic operations are meaningful, but only 150 is the defined equal-leg mean of ratios. Recomputing the index from the aggregate labels changes the definition.

An index of 110 therefore supports a specific descriptive statement: the selected boat has a 10 percent average relative advantage for that dimension under the admitted legs, peers and weighting. It does not prove 10 percent more talent,10 percent greater winning probability or a causal benefit of a tactical choice. A six-axis profile can be complete while only four of six legs support its evidence. Inspect coverage seconds, excluded endpoints and the peer list.

The actual fleet case, Naples practice race 1, has rich replay and analytical evidence. The match final illustrates partial support even for a calculated winner. A September 23 practice case illustrates what happens when completed windows cannot be established. These are useful teaching cases because they make the evidence boundary visible. Missing values protect interpretation. Replacing them with zero or invented passage times would make a prettier chart and a worse scientific claim.

Forecasting asks three separate questions. The live forecast asks who will win from a causally available on-water checkpoint. The pre-race forecast asks for expected finishing position over the full scheduled field. The Cup forecast combines stages, formats, classes, team strength assumptions and rule handling. Their targets, inputs and denominators differ, so a favorable evaluation for one does not certify the others.

Causal information means information that was available by prediction time. Observation time alone is insufficient. A weather measurement taken at 9:55 but published at 10:05 cannot inform a 10:00 prediction. Future gate records, final classifications and later revised products can leak outcome information if their availability is ignored. A chronological backtest must reconstruct what was knowable at each cutoff.

The current baseline path uses a provisional correlated Monte Carlo model. Correlated simulation matters because boats share conditions and relative strength uncertainty persists. Independent noise for every boat or every match changes rank and series behavior. A fixed uncertain strength can make two wins more likely than multiplying marginal probabilities. The model card and exact scenario path tell you which method was actually used; implemented alternatives are not necessarily published methods.

Completion also changes the target. In the scheduled-field expected-position convention a nonfinisher receives the field size N. Several boats can tie there. An expectation conditional on finishing is a different and often more favorable number. Removing absent telemetry boats from the field silently changes the problem. Hiding a boat in the interface should never shrink the scheduled denominator.

Weather support is not evidence of a learned response. Supported wind speeds can currently give identical expectations because the conservative reference uses a zero effect. Unsupported direction offsets remain unavailable. A successful exact-condition request proves that the workflow retained the requested conditions and returned a publication, not that a new wind effect has been validated.

The Cup publication has 5,000 trials per scenario and nine scenarios. In the baseline,3,277 paths are resolved and 1,723 are unresolved:34.46 percent. If a team wins w resolved paths, a conservative interval runs from w divided by 5,000 to w plus 1,723 divided by 5,000. Each upper endpoint assigns unresolved mass to a different team. Those upper endpoints cannot all happen together. They are not a probability vector and cannot honestly be normalized into one.

The outer envelope adds the tested assumptions. Its endpoints can come from different scenarios. Transfer from AC40 to AC75 is not a measured cross-class correlation in this archive. More simulations reduce numerical Monte Carlo noise but do not identify that transfer or resolve a missing tournament rule. Keep numerical error, model uncertainty and structural uncertainty separate.

How should you assess trust? Start with source identity and coverage. Then inspect the formula and a hand-checkable example. Then check export and interface consistency. Then ask whether the predictive target has enough independent validation. The full software gate passed for this snapshot, including 828 counted deterministic cases plus build and integrity commands. These checks establish implementation contracts, not calibration. The live descriptive audit has seven races over two days; the pre-race results do not uniformly beat the reference. That is a useful early result with a clear evidence ceiling.

The browser has five workspaces sharing race, playhead and boat state. Selecting a boat, changing leg scope and following a debrief moment or performance-leg interval should preserve the same evidence identity. Reports connects visible metrics to canonical exports. Local custom forecast generation adds a service, Python environment, prepared data and publication locks. Static HTML reading does not supply that backend. Grid works without map tiles when the application is locally served; the study pack itself works without any dashboard.

The next decisions should be driven by falsifiable questions. Independently audit complete scheduled outcomes and publication times. Compare frozen models on new race-days against simple references, with checkpoints averaged within race and days kept together. Test gap and gate sensitivity without hiding the deliberately removed observations. Acquire AC75 evidence before claiming measured transfer. Each experiment needs a success criterion and a failure criterion declared before seeing the answer.

Close the script and explain one value from memory. State its target, source, unit, window, denominator, cutoff and limitation. Trace it to a producer, an export and a UI location. Change one assumption and predict what should change. Name an observation that would make you retract your interpretation. That is the standard for being able to defend and improve this project independently.
