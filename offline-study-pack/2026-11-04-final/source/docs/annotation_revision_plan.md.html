Agent C: make Cup uncertainty intelligible and correct124125### What the current numbers mean126127Pre-change snapshot (2 November 2026): the active baseline uses 5,000 trials. Exactly 1,718 paths are unresolved and 3,282 are resolved. Conservative [0,1] allocation of each unresolved path gives every team's baseline interval a width of 34.36 percentage points. The nine transfer assumptions then widen the combined envelope. For ETNZ, baseline bounds are 17.04-51.40%, while the combined envelope is 13.84-67.08%.128129The grid is transfer loading {0,0.5,1} crossed with residual-uncertainty multiplier {0.5,1,2}. These are assumptions, not fitted cross-class correlations or posterior weights. The displayed Transfer scenarios range already includes unresolved-rule uncertainty. Its endpoints may arise from different scenarios. Marginal bound endpoints must not be normalized to sum to one.130131Documentation says final Cup scenarios use 100,000 draws, but the current build relies on the helper's 5,000 default. Make the budget explicit and correct the documentation. More draws address numerical simulation error; they cannot remove unresolved-rule or transfer uncertainty.132133### Correctness and lineage before display changes134135Preserve observed-versus-missing strength provenance. The public mapping currently emits a numerical zero for an unobserved Australian team, while the simulator detects missing history by absent keys. Audit the source model used to build Cup strength: the artifact must identify the exact model reference and training cutoff rather than implicitly inheriting the last loop model.136137Do not replace unresolved paths with an arbitrary lottery, a midpoint or a point probability conditional only on resolved paths.
