5. Fifteen Unresolved Questions Worth Thinking About on the Trip

  1. Two-Hull Syndicate Collapse (GBR B/G, ITA B/G, NZL B/G): In 2026 AC40 racing, three syndicates sailed two boats simultaneously (often splitting into an "A/Senior" crew and a "Youth/Women/Development" crew), and cup.py maps them to 2027 AC75 team strength by taking the unweighted arithmetic mean (β̂B + β̂G)/2. Would taking the maximum of the two hulls (or weighting by senior helmsman roster) better reflect single-boat AC75 capability?
  2. Equal-Leg vs Time/Distance-Weighted Peer Indices: performance-1 computes a dimension's race index as the unweighted mean of per-leg indices 1|L|∑_ℓ Ib,ℓ. If Leg 1 is a 240-second beat and Leg 5 is a shortened 80-second beat to the finish, should leg indices be weighted by leg distance or duration?
  3. Resolving the USA Start Conflict (pipeline/reconstruction.py:L228): If we fix comp.get("start_state", {}).get("started") in reconstruction.py so USA in naples_pr1 is classified as DNS/OCS instead of RANK_4, how should the replay UI visually distinguish a boat that physically sailed all 4 legs on the water (practice shadow sailing) from a legal competitor?
  4. Monotonic Gate-Time Filtering (26_09_22_nap_ipr_pr2): In historical_analysis.py, when a source leg_timings timestamp is non-monotonic (like USA Leg 3 in pr2), the current code rejects Leg 3 but uses Leg 3's backward timestamp as Leg 4's start time, causing Leg 2 and Leg 4 to overlap by 171.9 s. Should _leg_entry_for_boat enforce startms_ℓ = maxk < ℓ(endmsk) or fall back to reconstructed bow crossings whenever source leg_timings violate monotonicity?
  5. Tactical Wind Projection Gates (0 / 3,455 Active): All 7 projection gates in tactical_insights.py are well-motivated individually, but their conjunction suppresses 100% of 5-minute projections across all 28 races. Is a linear OLS trend over 120 s fundamentally the wrong functional form for oscillating Mediterranean sea-breeze shifts (where a damped sinusoid / AR(2) spectral peak might capture phase rather than linear drift)?
  6. Pre-Race Wind Speed Conditioning: Currently, export_pre_race_scenario uses the static joint_pl_reference model so expected positions are invariant to tws_kt. Since LegTimeModel already includes a log-TWS slope βtws, under what conditions would team-specific wind-speed interactions βtws, i become statistically identifiable without overfitting 9 race days?
  7. Practice vs Competitive Weighting (w = 0.5): StrengthModel weights practice fleet races at w = 0.5 relative to competitive fleet races (w = 1.0). In practice races, leading boats frequently peel off early on Leg 3 or Leg 4 to save equipment. Does w = 0.5 still allow intentional practice peel-offs to bias completion and leg-time models downward for top teams?
  8. RRSAC 102.1 Tie-Break Sensitivity: In cup_2027.json, 34.46% of baseline trials are unresolved because any tie in 6-race fleet points or 6-Challenger Round Robin standings halts exact point attribution. What if we separate non-pivot ties (e.g., a tie between 5th and 6th seed that only swaps Repechage 1 lane assignment) from pivot ties (a tie across the 3rd/4th direct-semifinal cutoff)? How much of the 34.46 pp width would collapse without inventing any tie-break rule?
  9. Manoeuvre Entry/Exit Window Asymmetry: ManoeuvreDetector defines entry speed over [-6.5s, -2.5s] before peak turn rate, whereas historical_analysis.py measures recovery up to +15.0s at a 95% threshold. When a boat tacks immediately into a wind header or mark-rounding bear-away, its optimal post-tack target speed changes — how can recovery metrics normalize for target polar speed rather than pre-tack entry speed?
  10. GNSS Attitude vs Heading-Only Bow Projection: _bow_point projects the +12.963m bowsprit in 2D using heading (ψ) assuming zero pitch and heel. During high-speed foiling or a splashdown bow-down pitch of 8°, how many centimetres does the horizontal projection of the bowsprit foreshorten, and could that affect photo-finish gate calls?
  11. Kalman Filter Turn-Rate Switching: CVKalmanFilter uses a constant-velocity model where acceleration noise q is isotropic. During an 18°/s foiling gybe, the boat experiences strong centripetal acceleration orthogonal to velocity. Would an Interacting Multiple Model (IMM) filter combining Constant-Velocity and Constant-Turn-Rate (CTRV) improve 10–30 s outage containment during manoeuvres?
  12. Separating Tactical Flyers from Boat Speed in LegTimeModel: In live simulation, LegTimeModel models remaining leg time as a function of scalar progress_m remaining. Two boats at the same progress_m — one in the center of the course and one on the extreme layline — have identical distance to go, but the layline boat must sail extra tacks/distance (cos(TWA) penalty). How can cross-track distance from the gate centerline be incorporated into effective remaining distance?
  13. Spatial Wind Field Non-Uniformity: windAt and tactical_insights.py treat wind on the course at timestamp t as a single spatially uniform vector (TWD(t), TWS(t)). Since all 9 AC40s log onboard twd and tws simultaneously across a 2km × 1km box, could we reconstruct a 2D spatial wind-pressure gradient across the course?
  14. Client-Side Bundle vs Lazy Data Architecture: As the archive grows from 28 races to 100+ races in 2027, public/data/analysis/index.json (2.1 MB) and .forecast/ artifacts will scale linearly. Where should pagination or IndexedDB caching be introduced in analysisCatalog.ts and predictionCatalog.ts?
  15. Epistemic Honesty as a Design Pattern: Most sports analytics dashboards hide missing data behind imputed numbers and forced 100% bar charts. This repository does the opposite — surfacing conflict, low_coverage, gate_ambiguous, and [lower, upper] rule bounds as first-class UI elements. Where else in quantitative engineering should this "refuse to fabricate precision" architecture be applied?
