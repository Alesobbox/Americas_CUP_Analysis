2. Ownership Boundaries & Five Runtime Phases

Before examining individual modules, keep the repository's strict architectural ownership rule in view (AGENTS.md, PROJECT.md):

  1. Python Pipeline Ownership (pipeline/, scripts/): - Owns all raw telemetry ingestion, coordinate projection, gate/start reconstruction, manoeuvre detection, historical metric calculation (historical_analysis.py), tactical wind-shift regression (tactical_insights.py), causal live/pre-race/Cup forecasting (pipeline/prediction/), and canonical CSV presentation tables (canonical_tables.py). - Writes static, immutable or schema-versioned JSON and CSV artifacts into public/data/.
  2. TypeScript Browser Ownership (src/): - Consumes public/data/ exports with strict runtime schema validation (analysisCatalog.ts, predictionCatalog.ts, manifest.ts). - Never recalculates historical metrics or forecast probabilities. - Owns only instantaneous 10 Hz display interpolation (ReplayEngine.ts, interpolate.ts), instantaneous live leaderboard & pairwise boat-to-boat geometry (src/engine/analytics.ts), 2D viewport projection & greedy label placement (ViewportMatrix.ts, CourseCanvas.tsx, boatPresentation.ts), and real-time convex-polygon contact rendering (contactDetector.ts).

Every module in the repository executes in one or more of five distinct runtime phases:

Subsystem S1 — Raw Telemetry Ingestion & Spatial Projection (pipeline/ingest.py, pipeline/projection.py)

  • Purpose & Conceptual Responsibility: Streams multi-gigabyte raw .jsonl race logs line-by-line in O(1) memory per record (iter_jsonl), extracts 11 record types (asset, boat_state, course, leg_timings, wind_summary, wind_history, autonomous_marks, mark_roundings, boundary, race_start_status, manoeuvres), resolves asset UUIDs to team codes (RM1–RM8 and competitor hulls), and projects WGS84 (lat, lon) into a local metric tangent plane (x East, y North) centered on a venue reference origin.
  • Important Files & Entry Points:
  • pipeline/ingest.py: IngestionPipeline (L444-L746), IngestionResult (L296-L441), iter_jsonl (L53-L69), _resolve_team_code (L126-L157).

  • Inputs, Outputs & Consumers:
  • Inputs: Raw Data/<session>.jsonl files (28 files, 6,866,006,601 bytes total).
  • Outputs: In-memory IngestionResult dataclass consumed by pipeline/__main__.py and scripts/ingest_regattas.py.
  • Dependencies & Execution Order: First stage of Phase 1 (pipeline/__main__.py:L40-L43). Must run before synchronization, reconstruction, manoeuvre detection, and analytics.
  • Tests Establishing Behavior: tests/unit/test_ingest.py, tests/unit/test_projection.py, tests/e2e/test_runner.py (Tier 1).
  • Lifecycle Status: Active (Core production pipeline).
  • Audit Note: IngestionResult.to_dict() (pipeline/ingest.py:L383-L388) hardcodes Naples projection constants in its intermediate dict summary, whereas the actual published replay manifest pipeline/export_replay.py:L153-L154 uses the session-specific converter (CAGLIARI_REFERENCE or NAPLES_REFERENCE). Subsystem S2 — 10 Hz Timeline Synchronization & Interpolation (pipeline/synchronize.py)

  • Purpose & Conceptual Responsibility: Resamples irregular per-boat boat_state streams (~10 Hz) and autonomous_marks streams (~1 Hz) onto a uniform 100 ms (10 Hz) master timeline spanning [window_start, window_end]. Uses linear interpolation for Cartesian coordinates and scalar channels (x, y, sog, vmg, tws, twa) and shortest-arc circular interpolation modulo 360° for directional bearings (heading, cog, twd). Clamps out-of-range timestamps to the nearest boundary observation (causing "held tail" plateaus when telemetry stops early).
  • Important Files & Entry Points:
  • pipeline/synchronize.py: TimelineSynchronizer (L127-L315), interpolate_angle_deg (L29-L48), interpolate_linear (L19-L26).
  • Inputs, Outputs & Consumers:
  • Inputs: Raw per-boat and per-mark telemetry lists from IngestionResult.
  • Outputs: SynchronizedTimeline containing aligned column arrays of identical length T at 100 ms steps, plus WindSample timeline. Consumed by reconstruction.py, manoeuvres.py, analytics.py, and export_replay.py.
  • Runtime Phase: Phase 1 (Ingestion & Replay Reconstruction).
  • Tests Establishing Behavior: tests/unit/test_synchronize.py, tests/tier5_integrity.py (test_timestamps_and_boat_columns_align).
  • Lifecycle Status: Active.

Subsystem S4 — Manoeuvre Detection (pipeline/manoeuvres.py)

  • Purpose & Conceptual Responsibility: Detects tacks and gybes from 10 Hz synchronized telemetry (heading, twa, sog, vmg) when raw manoeuvres records are absent (all 28 sessions have 0 raw manoeuvres records). Applies a 5-sample (0.5 s) circular moving average to heading, identifies turn-rate peaks (≥ 8.0°/s), verifies sign flip of TWA across ± 20°..160°, checks cumulative heading change ≥ 32°, enforces a speed dip ≥ 2.5kt (min_speed_loss_kt = 2.5), and classifies tacks (|TWA| < 90°) vs gybes (|TWA| ≥ 90°).
  • Important Files & Entry Points:
  • pipeline/manoeuvres.py: ManoeuvreDetector (L48-L246), detect_for_boat (L74-L227).
  • Inputs, Outputs & Consumers:
  • Inputs: 10 Hz boat telemetry arrays + gun_time + optional finish cutoff.
  • Outputs: List of ManoeuvreEvent records stored in replay.competitors[].manoeuvres and subsequently re-evaluated by historical_analysis.py (_event_analysis).
  • Runtime Phase: Phase 1 (Ingestion & Replay Reconstruction).
  • Tests Establishing Behavior: tests/unit/test_manoeuvres.py.
  • Lifecycle Status: Active (with an important dual-definition subtlety between replay export and historical analysis: manoeuvres.py:L192-L207 measures recovery to 90% of entry speed and caps unrecovered events at ~15.0 s, whereas historical_analysis.py:L294-L313 recomputes recovery from raw 10 Hz SOG to 95% of entry speed and sets unrecovered events to None).

Subsystem S8 — Causal Forecasting, Backtesting & 2027 AC38 Cup Simulator (pipeline/prediction/)

  • Purpose & Conceptual Responsibility: Implements three distinct probabilistic forecasting systems under strict forward-chaining temporal cutoffs (before_day, published_at_ms = state_timestamp_ms + 6000): 1. Pre-Race Expected Finish Position (pre-race-pl-mc-1): Empirical Bayes Beta-Binomial completion model (227 scheduled entries across 28 sessions) + Plackett–Luce finishing order (σ = 1.2, κ = 1.8) + tied-N nonfinish rank assignment. 2. Live In-Race Win Probability (live-mc-kalman-1): Causal 6,000 ms reorder buffer (ReorderBuffer), Constant-Velocity Kalman filter (CVKalmanFilter), log-normal leg-time remaining model (LegTimeModel) with correlated calendar-time weather noise (ρcalendar = 0.65), 500-trial exact boundary-crossing Monte Carlo, and 0.0001 uniform tail regularization. 3. 2027 America's Cup Winner Bounds (cup-ac38-conditional-2): 7-entrant AC38 tournament simulator (Preliminary Regattas, Challenger Selection Series RR/SF/Final, 1-point bonus carry-over, RRSAC 102.1 tie-break detection, and first-to-7 Cup Match) across a 24-scenario cross-class sensitivity grid (N = 5,000 trials/scenario), reporting [lower, upper] rule bounds rather than fabricated point probabilities because 34.46% of baseline trials encounter unpublished RRSAC 102.1 tie-break procedures.
  • Important Files & Entry Points:

AC40 Sailing / Class 40-foot (11.80 m hull + 1.163 m bowsprit = 12.963 m forward of transom, -0.215 m rudder aft, total length 13.178 m; foil span 10.83 m = ± 5.415m) one-design foiling monohull used in all 28 archived 2026 sessions. Defined in src/components/canvas/boatModels.ts:L28-L35.

Along-Leg Offset (alongLegM) Math / Compare UI Signed projection (in metres) of vector pB - pA onto the active leg direction of boat A: dirA · (Δ x sinθaxis + Δ y cosθaxis), where dirA = +1 on odd (upwind) legs and -1 on even (downwind) legs. Positive means Boat B is further ahead along A's current leg direction (src/engine/analytics.ts:L90-L92).

Circular Mean (_circular_mean_deg) Math / Directional Mean of angles θ1, …, θn ∈ [0°, 360°) computed via unit-vector components S = 1n∑ sinθi, C = 1n∑ cosθi as atan2(S, C) mod 360°. Undefined (None) when resultant length R = √S2 + C2 < 10-9 (pipeline/tactical_insights.py:L29-L41).
Closure Rate (closureMps) Math / Compare UI Rate (in m/s) at which the Euclidean separation between Boat A and Boat B is decreasing: -(vB - vA) · (pB - pA)\|pB - pA\|. Positive means boats are approaching each other; negative means separating (src/engine/analytics.ts:L97).
COG (cog) Sailing / Telemetry Course Over Ground in degrees clockwise from True North ([0°, 360°)). Direction of the boat's actual velocity vector over the water surface/seabed. Differs from heading (ψ) by leeway and current.
Contrast Subspace (contrast_cov) Math / Plackett–Luce The (K-1)-dimensional subspace of ℝK orthogonal to 1K (∑i=1K θi = 0) where Plackett–Luce log-strengths are identifiable. Covariance is computed via pseudoinverse H+ of the projected Hessian (pipeline/prediction/models.py:L226-L258).
Course Axis (axis_deg) Sailing / Geometry Bearing in degrees True ([0°, 360°)) from the leeward gate midpoint (or start midpoint on Leg 1) to the windward gate midpoint. Defines the primary upwind reference direction for VMG and Course camera rotation.
DNF / DNS / OCS / RET Sailing / Rules Non-finish classifications: DNF = Did Not Finish (started validly, did not cross finish before window end); DNS = Did Not Start (never crossed start line after gun); OCS = On Course Side (crossed start line early / flagged ocs: true without clearing); RET = Retired.
Gun Time (gun_time) Chronology Unix epoch timestamp in milliseconds (int) when the race start signal fired (T = 0s elapsed). Pre-start telemetry has t < guntime.
Gybe (GYBE) Sailing / Manoeuvre Turning the stern of the boat through the wind while sailing downwind (|TWA| ≥ 90°), flipping twa sign across the dead-downwind axis (180°).
Heading (heading, ψ) Sailing / Telemetry Direction the boat's bow points in degrees clockwise from True North ([0°, 360°)). Used to orient hull polygons and project the bowsprit tip.
Keep-Clear Border (keep_clear_border) Umpiring / Geometry Convex 9-vertex safety envelope expanded by +2.0m laterally around the physical platform_border (± 7.415m width for AC40, ± 10.0m for AC75). Overlap between two boats' keep_clear_border polygons triggers a virtual_contact_incident (src/engine/contactDetector.ts:L27-L38).
Lateral Offset (lateralM) Math / Compare UI Signed perpendicular offset (in metres) of Boat B relative to Boat A's course axis: Δ x cosθaxis - Δ y sinθaxis. Positive means Boat B lies to the right (eastward when θaxis = 0°) of the axis line through A (src/engine/analytics.ts:L93).
Matched-Leg Scope (matched_legs) Analytics / Radar Subset of race legs where both the subject boat and at least MIN_PEER_COUNT = 1 peer boat have valid (derived or verified) leg metrics. Prevents wind shifts on uncompleted legs from biasing performance-1 indices (pipeline/historical_analysis.py:L608-L654).
Normalized RPS (norm_rps) Forecast Evaluation Ranked Probability Score normalized by K-1 across finishing positions 1..K: 1K-1∑r=1K-1 (Fpred(r) - Factual(r))2, measuring cumulative distribution accuracy for ordinal rankings (pipeline/prediction/backtest.py:L60-L82).
Peer-Normalized Index (index) Analytics / Radar Dimensionless performance score where 100.0 represents the self-excluding peer mean. On leg ℓ, Ib,ℓ = 100 · xb,ℓ1|Pb,ℓ|∑p ∈ Pb,ℓ xp,ℓ. The race dimension index is the unweighted mean across matched legs: Ib = 1|Lb|∑ℓ ∈ Lb Ib,ℓ (pipeline/historical_analysis.py:L656-L712).

Accounts for 1,723 / 5,000 (34.46%) of unresolved baseline trials in cup_2027.json.
SOG (sog) Sailing / Telemetry Speed Over Ground in knots (kt, where 1kt = 1852 / 3600m/s ≈ 0.514444m/s).
Tack (TACK) Sailing / Manoeuvre Turning the bow of the boat through the wind while sailing upwind (|TWA| < 90°), flipping twa sign across 0°.
Tied-N Convention (nonfinish_score = "N") Pre-Race Forecast Scoring rule that assigns every non-finishing boat (DNS/DNF/OCS/RET) the worst scheduled field rank N = K (len(boat_ids)), whereas finishers receive ranks 1..m (m ≤ K). Under this convention, if ℙ(nonfinish) > 0, the sum of expected positions exceeds K(K+1)2.

TWD (twd) Sailing / Telemetry True Wind Direction in degrees clockwise from True North ([0°, 360°)) indicating the bearing from which the true wind blows.
TWS (tws) Sailing / Telemetry True Wind Speed in knots (kt).
VMG (vmg) Sailing / Analytics Velocity Made Good in knots (kt). Important Sign Distinction: In raw/replay telemetry (replay.telemetry.boats[id].vmg), vmg is wind-referenced (SOGcos(TWA)), which becomes negative downwind when |TWA| > 90°.
