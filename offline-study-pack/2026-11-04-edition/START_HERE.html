1. What to Open First (Laptop vs. Tablet / Phone)

On Your Laptop (Full Study Environment)

  1. Start Here: Open START_HERE.html in any browser (file:// works directly with Wi-Fi off).
  2. 15-Minute Mental Model: Open 02_Field_Guide.pdf (or 02_Field_Guide.html) to see the entire system on one page, the formula reference sheet, the three forecasting targets, and the troubleshooting decision tree.
  3. Deep Conceptual Mastery: Read 01_Repository_Explained.pdf alongside the searchable 01_Repository_Explained.html and the 209-file source-companion/index.html.
  4. Active Self-Testing (Closed-Book First!): Work through the 36 exercises in 04_Workbook.pdf before checking 04_Workbook_Solutions.pdf.
  5. Interactive Experimentation: Open examples/interactive_sailing_and_forecast_lab.html and study-materials/flashcards_viewer.html directly in your browser.

On Your Phone or E-Ink Tablet (Trail / Hut Reading)

Copy these five zero-dependency files to your tablet/phone reader: - 01_Repository_Explained.pdf — Complete 9-Part Textbook & Case Studies - 02_Field_Guide.pdf — Condensed 8–12 Page Companion - 04_Workbook.pdf — 36 Exercises Across 3 Difficulty Tiers - 04_Workbook_Solutions.pdf — Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivations & Answer Key - study-materials/06_Audio_Narration_Script.html — Spoken-Word Rehearsal Script (compatible with iOS/Android system text-to-speech)

  • Optional Full Repository Static Data Footprint:
  • Built frontend bundle (dist/): 2,412,191 bytes (2.41 MB).
