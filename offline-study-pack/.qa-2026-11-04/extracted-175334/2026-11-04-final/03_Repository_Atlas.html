Repository atlas and evidence index

This atlas describes the selected first-party reading snapshot of4 November2026. It maps conceptual responsibilities, execution order, inputs, consumers, status and tests. The actual source copy contains213 files; evidence/snapshot.json identifies their bytes and the dirty working tree. This is a reading set, not a complete Git clone or operational backup. No raw telemetry, dependency tree, credentials or prepared feature cache is bundled.

Execution order

Replay path: session identity → local projection and channel synchronization → crossing/leg/manoeuvre reconstruction → replay and historical exports → catalogs and browser. Forecast path branches from raw causal observations → sealed dataset → state and cutoff-aware labels → strength/completion models → correlated simulation or declared reference → tournament adaptation → validated immutable publication. Historical summaries are not causal live features. Environment acquisition and report/previews generation are separate refresh paths.

Cross domain glossary

Term or fieldMeaning and caution
SessionOne source race/session export; a filename is not a guaranteed date.
Boat-raceOne boat observed in one race; the historical unit is not a frame.
Scheduled entryA member of the scheduled forecast field, even without replay telemetry.
FrameA regular100 ms replay state, often interpolated.
GateA directed line between identified marks in an ordered course sequence.
LegA course segment bounded by start/checkpoint timing.
TackUpwind change of wind side, subject to detector context and coverage.
GybeDownwind change of wind side.
HeadingHull orientation bearing clockwise from north.
COGCourse over ground, the direction of motion.
SOG / sog_mean_ktSpeed over ground; its canonical mean needs an eligible source-supported window.
TWDTrue wind direction from which wind comes.
TWSTrue wind speed, with source and units retained.
TWADerived wind angle relative to heading; not interchangeable with velocity angle.
Course-axis VMGVelocity projected onto the course axis; raw sign can differ by leg direction.
Wind-relative VMGVelocity projection relative to wind, with its own sign convention.
x,yLocal east,north metres with exported origin and coefficients.
timestamp_msInteger milliseconds; distinguish seconds in durations.
Checkpoint gapDifference in passage times at a shared checkpoint.
Continuous gapA moving geometry/speed estimate, not a checkpoint observation.
OCSStart-side/source-state concept; a visual early intersection alone is insufficient.
DNSDid not start; preserve field membership and missing telemetry separately.
RET / DNFRetired/nonfinish labels with source and evidence limits.
Calculated resultReconstructed classification, not independently verified official results.
qualityProvenance/availability category such as derived, reconstructed, partial or unavailable.
coverage_sSupported seconds under the owning metric definition.
sample_countEvent/leg or other defined sample count; not necessarily telemetry frames.
matched_legsEligible same-leg comparisons admitted into an index.
peer_meanEqual-boat other-boat baseline within eligible scope.
peer_idsNamed contributing boats; selected boat excluded.
index100 times selected/peer ratio, averaged equally over eligible legs.
overall_indexAggregate defined only under required dimension availability; not ability.
retentionMinimum versus entry-speed ratio over selected eligible detected events.
debriefRanked observed review items with replay links, not causal advice.
tactical_insightsExported recorded trend snapshots; projections remain gated.
schema_versionStructural contract version, distinct from formula/method version.
method_versionVersion of scientific calculation or export method.
Historical ViewIndex/cohort view over boat-races, not frames.
A / BPrimary selection and explicit comparison selection; not forecast team rankings.
Grid / MapDirect metre canvas versus geographic base; Grid avoids tile requests.
EnhancedReadable display scaling, separate from physical contact calculation.
Keep ClearModeled safety envelope; overlap is not an official ruling.
training_cutoffOnly prior available evidence may fit the model.
available_atInformation publication/availability time, distinct from observation time.
Plackett-LuceSequential positive-weight ranking reference.
correlated_mcJoint Monte Carlo candidate with shared uncertainty, currently provisional.
NComplete scheduled field size; nonfinish score convention uses N.
expected_positionUnconditional expected scheduled-field score including nonfinish.
BrierSummed multiclass squared probability error under this evaluator.
Log lossNegative logarithm of probability assigned to the observed winner.
RPSRanked probability score for an ordered distribution, normalized by contract.
CalibrationEmpirical frequency agreement requiring independent outcomes.
PseudoreplicationTreating dependent repeated measurements as independent evidence.
Monte Carlo errorNumerical finite-trial sampling uncertainty conditional on a model.
Transfer loadingAssumed AC40-to-AC75 strength relationship, not measured correlation.
Rule boundConservative allocation of unresolved tournament mass.
Scenario envelopeCoordinatewise extremes across assumptions; endpoints can differ in origin.
model_refCanonical model-card content hash.
source_manifest_hashCanonical manifest identity; does not imply all raw bytes rehashed today.
scenario_hashDeclared scenario input identity, distinct from byte SHA of a file.
Immutable objectContent-addressed publication retained after active index changes.
Atomic indexValidated pointer switch using replacement, with prior publication surviving failures.
Publication lockShared local job ownership guard including publisher child process.
Static hostingCan display saved objects; new generation needs the prepared local service.

Evidence routes and preservation

Start with frozen source navigation, the snapshot manifest, bounded actual case evidence, current model/evaluation findings, UI findings, and fresh verification. Source citations above point to entry points; detailed line ranges in the textbook point to producing functions. Existing notices are preserved. No top-level repository license or independent official-result verification is invented.
