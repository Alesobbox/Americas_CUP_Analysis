1# America's Cup AC40 replay dashboard23Replay and historical analysis for 28 sessions: nine Cagliari races and nineteen Naples races, containing 224 boat-race tracks.45## Dashboard views67- **Replay:** boat and moving-mark tracks, wind, playhead-aware Tactical insights, reconstructed events, standings, telemetry, and local race notes.8- **Analysis:** a white workspace with exported metrics, coverage, team history, interactive circular performance comparisons and a replay-linked Team debrief. Choose a dimension and leg to inspect exact peer evidence. Python owns historical calculations.9- **Compare:** two explicitly selected boats at the shared playhead.10- **Reports:** canonical race metrics, summary/performance CSVs and concise race downloads, plus a collapsed searchable technical archive.11- **Forecast:** experimental race forecasts and gated event/Cup evidence, with separate baseline rule bounds, transfer scenarios and numerical error.1213Map mode uses OpenStreetMap with attribution. Offline Grid mode uses a 250 m metric grid without tile requests. True metric scale and enhanced visibility are available in Settings. Unsupported channels explain their unavailability.
