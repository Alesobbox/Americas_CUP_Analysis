1# Browser feedback implementation and verification
2
3Verified on 4 November 2026 against Naples Practice Race 1 at replay time 240 seconds, including the reported 775 × 704 viewport. This extends the [visual redesign](ui_redesign_verification.md).
4
5## Delivered behavior
6
7- Boats default to an enhanced visible size. Every on-screen boat has a readable team label and optional signed course-axis VMG, with leader lines to its actual position. The selected A has a solid blue border; an explicitly highlighted B has a dashed purple border. Labels are placed away from one another, vessel anchors, course badges and the lower wind display where space allows. True metric scale remains selectable; physical boat and contact geometry are unchanged.
8- Trail visibility supports All boats, Highlighted boats and No trails. Highlighted means A plus the explicitly enabled B. Trail colour supports team, speed and VMG; length is available under Trail settings & boat B. All boats and the complete fleet ranking remain visible when only highlighted trails are drawn.
9- Grid/Map switching preserves the current metric scale and center, including the first switch from Grid. The map camera derives its zoom from the current viewport rather than a stale cached zoom. A browser regression checks scale, center and all nine labels at 775 pixels.
10- Forecast can generate exact wind-speed scenarios through the local dashboard, show progress, retry failures and load the published result. Changing conditions while preparation is running clears stale display state and permits retrying the original inputs. Development and local preview share the same workspace publication lock, including publisher-child ownership.
11- The current forecasting model has no validated direction response. Nonzero offsets, including 17°, are explained before submission. Use 0° offset is an explicit action; there is no silent condition substitution. Unsupported historical coverage still produces an unavailable reason. Supported speeds can have identical expectations because the current model uses a conservative zero-effect wind reference.
12- Reports uses Result, and visible quality/provenance wording uses Calculated or Calculated from telemetry. Source quality codes and downloads retain their provenance. The note that finishing classifications have not been independently verified remains visible. An expanded-disclosure audit found no occurrence of the old wording in the five workspaces.
13- Performance uses a solid selected profile, dashed peer baseline and an explicit legend. Six signed difference rows show the exported index relative to 100, with above/below-mean wording, a shared difference scale and linked evidence. The absolute readings, eligible peer baseline, leg scope, missing values and coverage remain available. Historical indexes are not recalculated in the browser.
