Before departure

Extract the entire ZIP. Copy both the ZIP and extracted directory to your laptop. For a phone/tablet, copy the PDFs or the entire extracted pack for the source companion and HTML. Test with Wi-Fi disabled. The four PDFs and every HTML reading page work without installation. The standard-library concept lab needs Python3; saved output is included. No audio was generated; use the narration script with a device read-aloud feature.

Size and scope
