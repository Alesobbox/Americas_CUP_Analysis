3. Important Quantities, Formulas, Units & Sign Conventions

3.1 Coordinate Systems, Projection & Vessel Geometry

  1. WGS84 Ellipsoidal Radii of Curvature (pipeline/projection.py:L29-L68): With semi-major axis a = 6,378,137.0m, flattening f = 1/298.257223563, e2 = 2f - f2, and reference latitude ϕ0:

    M(ϕ0) = a(1 - e2)(1 - e2 sin2ϕ0)3/2, N(ϕ0) = a√1 - e2 sin2ϕ0
    x = (λ - λ0)π180 N(ϕ0)cosϕ0 (m East), y = (ϕ - ϕ0)π180 M(ϕ0) (m North)
    * Naples Origin (NAPLES_REFERENCE): (ϕ0, λ0) = (40.80875^∘N, 14.21565^∘E) ⟹ 84,282.58m/deg lon, 111,050.25m/deg lat. * Cagliari Origin (CAGLIARI_REFERENCE): (ϕ0, λ0) = (39.19000^∘N, 9.14250^∘E) ⟹ 86,383.48m/deg lon, 111,019.66m/deg lat.

  2. Nautical Compass vs Standard Math Angles: * All bearings (heading ψ, cog, twd, axis_deg) are degrees clockwise from True North (0^∘ = North, 90^∘ = East). * Unit vector along bearing θ: (sinθ, cosθ) in (xEast, yNorth), not (cosθ, sinθ)!

  3. Vessel-Local to World Transform (src/engine/contactDetector.ts:L101-L118): For local starboard coordinate u and forward coordinate v relative to the transom GNSS origin (x0, y0) at heading ψ:

    xworld = x0 + ucosψ + vsinψ, yworld = y0 - usinψ + vcosψ
    In particular, the AC40 bowsprit tip (u = 0, v = Lbow = +12.963m) is at:
    pbow(t) = \bigl(x(t) + 12.963sinψ(t), y(t) + 12.963cosψ(t)\bigr)

3.2 Interpolation, Crossings & Critical Sign Conventions

  1. Shortest-Arc Circular Interpolation (pipeline/synchronize.py:L29-L48, src/engine/interpolate.ts:L7-L10):

    Δθ = \bigl((θ1 - θ0 + 540^∘) \bmod 360^∘\bigr) - 180^∘ ∈ [-180^∘, +180^∘), θ(α) = (θ0 + α Δθ) \bmod 360^∘

  2. Sub-Frame Line-Segment Intersection (pipeline/reconstruction.py:L39-L73): For bow motion p0 → p1 (r = p1 - p0) and gate segment q0 → q1 (s = q1 - q0) with 2D cross product a × b = ax by - ay bx:

    u = (q0 - p0) × sr × s ∈ [0, 1], v = (q0 - p0) × rr × s ∈ [0, 1], tcross = round\bigl(t0 + u(t1 - t0)\bigr)

  3. CRITICAL SIGN CONVENTION — Two Different Definitions of VMG: * Replay Telemetry vmg (replay.telemetry.boats[id].vmg): Wind-referenced projection SOGcos(TWA). Positive upwind (|TWA| < 90^∘), NEGATIVE downwind (|TWA| > 90^∘). * Historical Analysis avg_vmg_kt & upwind_vmg/downwind_vmg (public/data/analysis/*.json): Mark/axis-referenced projection along the active leg unit vector ûleg. POSITIVE on both upwind and downwind legs whenever the boat makes progress toward the target gate!

  4. 6-Axis Self-Excluding Peer-Normalized Index (performance-1) (pipeline/historical_analysis.py:L580-L746): For boat b on matched leg \ell ∈ Lb with valid peer set Pb,\ell = p ≠ b : xp,\ell valid (|Pb,\ell| ≥ 1):

    x-b,\ell = 1|Pb,\ell|∑p ∈ Pb,\ell xp,\ell, Ib,\ell = 100 · xb,\ellx-b,\ell, Ib,d = 1|Lb,d|∑\ell ∈ Lb,d Ib,\ell
    overallindexb = 1|Db|∑d ∈ Db Ib,d
    Note: Because Ib,d is the mean of per-leg ratios (1|L|∑_\ell 100 xb,\ellx-b,\ell), it does not equal 100 × valuebpeermeanb (the ratio of cross-leg means) when leg speeds vary across legs!

5. The Most Consequential Assumptions & Limitations

  1. Zero Official Finishing Orders (official_order_verified = False): Every race result, finishing rank, and elapsed time in the archive is reconstructed from GNSS telemetry and autonomous mark streams, not signed race-committee sheets.
  2. Small Effective Sample Size (28 Sessions / 9 Race Days vs 331,089 Frames): High-frequency 10 Hz sampling reduces measurement noise within a leg, but provides zero additional degrees of freedom for cross-race team skill estimation or weather regression.
  3. Unidentifiable 2027 AC38 Rules (34.46% Unresolved Baseline Trials): Because AC38 Notice of Race / RRSAC 102.1 multi-way tie-break procedures and AC40-to-AC75 correlation ρ are unobserved, 1,723 / 5,000 baseline Cup trials cannot be uniquely resolved without inventing rules.
  4. Two-Boat Fleet Collapse (GBR and ITA Red/Black Hulls): In 2026 AC40 preliminary sessions, Great Britain (GBR B, GBR G), Italy (ITA B, ITA G), and New Zealand (NZL B, NZL G) fielded two hulls simultaneously, whereas the 2027 Cup is a single-hull AC75 event per syndicate.
  5. Pre-Race Wind Invariance & Inactive Delay Mixture: In pipeline/prediction/models.py, PlackettLuceModel and CompletionModel do not include a tws_kt covariate (so pre-race expected positions are identical across 8..22 kt), and PositiveDelayMixture is fit on empty delay lists (available = False).
  6. Conservative Tactical Projection Suppression (0 / 3,455 Projections Emitted): pipeline/tactical_insights.py applies 7 sequential quality gates before emitting a 5-minute wind-shift projection; in the current archive, 100% of snapshots are suppressed at the projection stage (projection_change_deg = null).

6. Common Interpretation Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

# Common Mistake Why It Is False Correct Interpretation
1 Treating 331,089 synchronized frames as 331,089 independent samples. Consecutive 100 ms frames are strongly autocorrelated (and interpolated from ~10 Hz boat / ~1 Hz mark packets). Always count boat-races (224 replayed / 227 scheduled) or matched legs as the statistical denominator.
2 Expecting 100 * value / peer_mean to equal dimension.index in performance-1. dimension.index is the mean of per-leg ratios, whereas value and peer_mean are cross-leg means. By Jensen's inequality / covariance of ratios, 1|L|∑ x_\elly_\ell ≠ xy (e.g., ITA G downwind VMG in naples_pr1 is 109.97 vs 108.09).
3 Assuming a peer index of 110 means "10% faster than the entire 9-boat fleet". The baseline excludes non-finishers on unmatched legs (matched_legs scope) and excludes the subject boat itself (Pb,\ell). Read scope.matched_legs and leg_evidence[].peer_count: in a 2-boat match race (naples_mr_final), peer_count = 1, so index is simply 100 × xA / xB on completed legs 1..4.
4 Averaging raw telemetry vmg across upwind and downwind legs. Telemetry vmg is negative downwind (SOGcos(TWA)). Use mark/axis-referenced VMG from analysis/<race_id>.json (avg_vmg_kt), which is positive toward the active mark on all legs.
5 Expecting pre-race expected positions to sum to K(K+1)2 = 45 in a 9-boat race. The forecast uses the tied-N convention (nonfinish_score = "N"), assigning rank K to every non-finisher. Whenever ℙ(nonfinish) > 0, non-finishers receive rank K instead of the average of bottom ranks, strictly increasing ∑ 𝔼[Ri] above K(K+1)2.
6 Normalizing the 2027 Cup rule_bounds_by_team midpoints or upper bounds to sum to 100%. The width u = upperi - loweri = 0.3446 is shared epistemic mass from unresolved RRSAC 102.1 tie-break trials. Never normalize interval endpoints; doing so secretly imposes an arbitrary tie-break rule that the repository explicitly refuses to fabricate.
7 Treating virtual_contact_incidents (COLLISION / PROXIMITY) as official umpire penalties. Contacts are pure 2D convex-polygon SAT overlaps of 10 Hz GNSS hulls (keep_clear_border / platform_border). Telemetry has ∼ 1–2m GNSS/heading jitter and no umpire protest flags; treat contacts as geometric proximity candidates.
8 Assuming constant coordinates at the end of a replay mean the boat anchored on the course. TimelineSynchronizer clamps timestamps after a boat's last packet to its final coordinate (held tail). Check tail_held_s in analysis/<race_id>.json (e.g., GBR B in naples_pr1 has a 951.55 s held tail after retiring).
