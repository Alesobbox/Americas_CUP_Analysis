1# Team analytics23Added 1 November 2026. These features extend the existing dashboard design and downloadable historical details. Python owns their formulas and availability; the browser only selects snapshots, formats values, and draws scores.45## Where to use them67- **Replay / Compare:** Tactical insights follows the shared playhead. Rewind selects an earlier snapshot; a future or expired observation is never displayed as current.8- **Analysis:** Select a race and boat, choose all comparable legs or a single leg, then activate a circular-chart dimension to pin exact exported evidence. Expand a contributing leg to inspect peers and review its interval in Replay. Team debrief lists further review priorities.9- **Reports:** Use the race summary and boat/leg performance CSVs or the full analysis JSON. The collapsed Technical data and provenance explorer retains every published object.1011## Tactical insights: tactical-1, schema 1.01213The input consists of independent raw official-wind direction and speed observations with their original measurement timestamps. It does not use the replay's interpolated wind, a retrospective whole-race baseline, boat-local wind as a substitute, or an external weather service.1415Snapshots are exported every 10 seconds from the gun through the replay window end. A session that ended before its gun has an empty snapshot grid with an explicit reason. Each uses a trailing 120-second window. A one-second grid takes the most recent observation at or before each grid instant, with a maximum source age of two seconds. A direction or speed window requires at least 60 source samples, every grid instant covered, and no source interval longer than two seconds. Missing direction is unavailable rather than north/zero. Direction and speed availability are independent.1617Direction is unwrapped using shortest circular differences. The observed direction change is the end-minus-start unwrapped angle; positive means clockwise (veering), negative counterclockwise (backing). The direction rate is the ordinary least-squares slope on the one-second trailing grid, in degrees per minute. Speed change is the difference between fresh endpoint speed observations, in knots. Coverage counts one-second intervals with fresh endpoints and no overlap with a rejected source gap. Direction and speed each export their own coverage, source sample count, quality and missing-data reason.1819A conditional five-minute projection is five times the recent direction rate. It requires at least a one-degree net change, absolute rate of at least 0.2 degrees/minute, projected change no greater than 30 degrees, consistent nonzero rate signs across the two one-minute halves, half-window rates within max(0.25 degrees/minute, 50% of the full-window rate), residual RMS at most max(1 degree, 20% of net change), and no one-second directional jump over three degrees. These are deterministic suppression rules, not learned or validated forecast gates. A projection states the assumption that the recent trend continues; it has no calibrated probability, interval, or promised arrival time. Variable, flat or steep windows explain why a projection is unavailable.2021The browser selects the latest snapshot at or before the playhead, verifies the race ID, and expires it after 12 seconds. Before the first snapshot, it explicitly reports that no window has been reached. Observation and projection are labelled separately.2223Source provenance records the file, SHA-256 and official-wind asset ID. Causality is by measurement timestamp for retrospective replay. Raw records can arrive out of order; arrival-time availability is not established, and adding late records with earlier timestamps can revise historical snapshots. Hashes identify the exact source used for this export.2425## Boat performance: performance-1, schema 1.02627Six dimensions describe this race: upwind and downwind leg-directed course VMG, upwind and downwind mean SOG, and detector-conditioned tack and gybe speed retention. Odd legs follow the existing upwind course-axis convention, and even legs its reciprocal. There is no measured polar or optimal-speed target, so these are relative observed-performance indexes.2829A boat is excluded for OCS, retirement, DNS, a missing start crossing, or a flagged source-state conflict. A non-finisher may contribute its valid completed legs. Speed and VMG require positive finite values, a completed leg, derived quality and at least 95% valid source coverage over the reconstructed leg duration.3031For each eligible boat and leg, the baseline is the equal-weight mean of **other eligible boats on that exact leg**. The leg index is `100 * boat_value / peer_mean`. The dimension index is the arithmetic mean of its matched-leg indexes; every matched leg has one vote. A peer boat has one vote per leg. Displayed raw boat values and peer means are arithmetic matched-leg means, so their ratio need not equal the mean of per-leg ratios. Evidence retains the per-leg values, indexes, peer identities, sample counts and coverage. Dimension peer count is the unique union of contributing other boats; the exact count on each leg is available in the JSON evidence.3233Speed retention is `100 * minimum_SOG / entry_SOG` for qualifying detected manoeuvres. Each boat's same-kind events are averaged within a completed leg before comparison with the other boats. The full detector source window, 65 replay samples before through 60 samples after the timestamp, must be inside the leg and valid for SOG, heading and local TWD, without source gaps. At the default 100 ms cadence this is 6.5 seconds before through six seconds after; at 200 ms it is 13 seconds before through 12 seconds after. Nondefault regular grids record `race.sample_interval_ms`; omitted cadence means the existing 100 ms default. Events near gate roundings are excluded. The detector requires a speed reduction of at least 2.5 knots, so this describes its qualifying event population rather than every real tack or gybe. Coverage is the union of cadence-scaled validated event support windows, avoiding overlap double-counting; debrief manoeuvre source checks use the same cadence.3435Index 100 is the other-boats baseline; higher indicates a larger observed value for every dimension. An overall index uses an equal-weight average only when all six dimensions are available and is labelled a heuristic profile summary. Missing dimensions remain null with a reason, and the chart leaves gaps. The graph uses one shared full-range scale for all boats and legs in the race, starting at 0–150 and expanding when necessary. A focused detail plot makes small differences around 100 visible; full arithmetic remains inspectable. This comparison does not control for changing wind, start times, traffic or tactical obligations, and establishes no intrinsic yacht/crew ranking.3637The additive `scope` evidence records expected and matched course legs, excluded-leg reasons, minimum peer count and source-covered seconds. Coverage counts the union of contributing leg intervals once, avoiding duplicate SOG/VMG support. Each dimension's `leg_evidence` now also includes interval boundaries, per-peer values/coverage/quality/counts, excluded-peer reasons and qualifying manoeuvre events. No performance-1 formula changed. Thirteen profiles with six available dimensions cover only part of their course: the match-final ITA B profile compares legs 1–4 of six, with 847.085 source-covered seconds. Missing or inconsistent source crossings remain excluded.3839## Team debrief: debrief-1, schema 1.04041The queue contains up to three largest positive leg-time differences from the mean of other eligible boats on that same completed and sufficiently covered leg, plus up to two covered detected manoeuvres with the largest entry-to-minimum SOG reductions. Items retain units, source, quality, timestamps and peer identities where applicable. A leg-time difference is an observed deficit; manoeuvre speed reduction is neither seconds lost nor distance lost. Replay links support analyst review; neither item identifies a causal explanation.4243## Refresh and compatibility4445Run `npm run analytics` to stream source wind and refresh all race extensions, the historical index and the artifact inventory. Replay telemetry arrays, existing historical scalar values, historical schema `2.0` / method `historical-2`, event-feed schema `1.0` / method `event-feed-1`, Forecast and historical environment artifacts remain compatible.
