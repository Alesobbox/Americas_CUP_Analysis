State and propagation4950Use state `s=(x,y,vx,vy)` in metres and m/s. COG, not boat heading, defines the measured ground-velocity direction. For elapsed time dt, use the continuous-white-acceleration constant-velocity model:5152```text53F(dt) = [[I, dt I], [0, I]]54Q(dt) = q [[dt^3/3 I, dt^2/2 I], [dt^2/2 I, dt I]]55m_minus = F m56P_minus = F P F' + Q57```5859Here q is acceleration spectral density, not the variance of a position random walk. Position uncertainty includes propagated velocity uncertainty and cubic process-noise growth. The review's `sigma_position^2 = sigma0^2 + q*dt` is appropriate for a different position-random-walk model; it is not this filter's covariance equation.6061Update position/ground velocity only when their timestamped measurements are available. Estimate positive measurement/process scales from earlier source innovations, distinguishing steady/turning windows by causal turn-rate summaries. Use robust fitting and nonzero covariance floors; no fabricated zero GPS error. Keep source event time, filter update/publication time and replay watermark separate. Never run a backward smoother into the prediction cutoff.6263During missing measurements, sample `(x,y,vx,vy)` from its filtered predictive distribution once per race continuation. Do not also add the same state covariance as independent time-noise. Label quality `measured`, `estimated`, `gate_ambiguous` or `unavailable` and export source age. Filtered state is a modeling assumption, not a new measurement.6465### A2. Certification and stopping rules6667Create disjoint artificial gaps of 2/5/10/20/30/60/90 seconds within otherwise well-covered earlier races. Test position-only loss and loss of both position/velocity; stratify steady motion, turns, recovery and gate approaches. Use held-out source positions only as scoring targets. Keep all tuning inside past data.6869Preregister the following engineering gates: at least 50 nonoverlapping masks and two earlier dates for a certified stratum; at least 90% empirical containment by the claimed 95% position ellipse; median position error below 10% of leg length; and current 95% major radius at most min(50 metres, 10% of leg length). These thresholds are risk policies, not confidence guarantees. Report counts and containment by date/stratum, rejection/availability rates and natural-gap error. A mask failing a phase test is not removed to improve calibration scores.7071Choose the largest contiguous tested duration prefix passing the gates. No certification beyond 90 seconds. Early folds without enough evidence use the declared reference procedure; they do not borrow later dates. Inspect the 74.6-second natural dropout separately: it may remain unavailable. A long extrapolation is never justified merely because its mean looks plausible.7273Do not confirm legal starts/gates from propagation alone. If predictive state permits different gate/finish histories, export that ambiguity. The MVP declines a numeric live forecast when the field's necessary phase cannot be resolved; the competitor remains registered. A broad history-conditioned missing-state mixture is Phase 2 and requires its own masked-gap end-to-end validation. This replaces common short-gap blackouts without pretending every outage is recoverable.7475### A3. Marks and outcome certainty7677Marks have a separate slow-position random walk: hold the last causal mean and grow covariance as `P_mark + Q_mark*dt`, with Q fitted from earlier movement. A 30-second ceiling is reduced if masked-mark tests fail. A relocation/course revision invalidates the old hold. Sample endpoint uncertainty when computing line/gate geometry. A target/configured position is labelled configured, not measured.7879If a gate/finish is observed, resolve its event time before its publication time. The first received finish message is not necessarily the first on-water finisher. A terminal 100% value requires the global on-water ordering to be resolved, including any missing opponent. This prevents state estimation from manufacturing a winner.8081Primary background: [Sinopoli et al., Kalman filtering with intermittent observations](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/papers/sinopoli-etal.pdf). Its linear/Gaussian setting supports the propagation principle, not a claim that a CV model predicts unseen tacks accurately. Different process-noise conventions must not be mixed; see [motion-model conventions](https://www.mathworks.com/help/fusion/ug/motion-model-state-and-process-noise.html).8283## B. Geometry and live kinematic features8485Let the vector from the boat to a gate point be d, the unit upwind axis from the causal circular TWD be e, and its perpendicular be e_perp. Use `a=d dot e`, `b=d dot e_perp`, distances to both endpoints, gate width/angular width and current tack as empirical features. Reverse the progress axis for downwind legs. Flag weak/variable wind direction rather than normalizing an unstable vector without warning.8687The review's distance expression is not adopted. At a=100 m, b=0 and theta=145 degrees, it gives approximately -52 m. Downwind angles require a different axis convention. Even for 0<theta<90 degrees, two fixed allowed headings +/-theta require `abs(b)<=a*tan(theta)` and imply total length `a/cos(theta)` inside that cone; outside it, the same fixed-heading model is infeasible. Adding an unscaled crosswind remainder does not establish a new feasible route. Without a supported empirical speed/angle model, export geometric features, not an asserted layline distance.8889Causal features include SOG, a trailing 10-second acceleration slope, circular turn rate, time since a detected past turn, and speed relative to earlier steady-window speed. Wind shift is the wrapped difference between 30- and 120-second circular means. A signed header/lift interpretation additionally needs tack and leg context. Values such as 12 seconds, 0.85 speed ratio and 11 knots may be review heuristics, never evidence of foil height. The existing manoeuvre detector's future stability/recovery windows may create training labels only after availability; they cannot supply live flags directly.9091For the ranking benchmark, test `lead_fraction/sqrt(max(remaining_fraction,0.01))` against raw lead. Brownian score models motivate a denominator proportional to sqrt(remaining time) under drift/noise assumptions; distance is an approximation. Do not infer universal sailing physics or infinite certainty from this feature. [Stern's Brownian sports-score model](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/157/Papers/stern.pdf) is an analogue, not AC40 validation.9293## C. Positive-delay mixture and gate consistency9495### C1. Units, interpretation and fitting9697Use the two-component candidate:9899```text100T_clean ~ LogNormal(mu(context, crew), sigma_clean^2)101Z ~ Bernoulli(pi(context))102delay | Z=1 ~ Gamma(shape, scale_seconds)103T = T_clean + Z*delay104```105106All durations/delays are seconds. If a multiplicative log-time mixture is tested instead, its jump must be dimensionless. Do not add a 25-second term to log(T). Z means one or more severe performance-loss episodes aggregated for that leg; it is not an observed foil-state label. A recovered speed loss is not a terminal DNF.107108Start with pooled pi and pooled delay parameters, sharing conditional wind/leg effects with strong regularization. Add wind/recovery dependence only if the fixed comparison supports it; no hard 9-knot onset. Fit the clean and delay components jointly, anchored where reviewed clean/loss examples exist. The single-lognormal model is a reference competitor, not a fallback labelled physically true. Do not fit on fast-only selected data and then claim competitive unconditional tails.109110Review episodes using speed loss, turn history, local/fleet wind, duration, recovery and coverage. Use the empirical component name until that evidence exists.111112Accept the mixture only if component parameters and allocations are stable across date deletion/initialization, the delay component is identifiable from reviewed loss examples rather than duplicate means, and held-out gate-time tails plus final race scores improve within P3 budgets. [Mixtures-of-experts review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08200) provides the statistical background, not sailing-specific threshold evidence.113114### C2. No double counting and current recovery115116The mixture replaces the existing leg residual distribution. Do not add an event delay to a model already fitted to total loss-inclusive leg times without refitting that baseline. Do not additionally sample the same event through residual blocks, a foundation quantile spread and a separate tack-cost process.117118At a live checkpoint, distance/progress and elapsed time already reflect losses incurred. Condition on current recovery evidence and sample only **future residual loss**. Recovery duration and added gate-arrival delay are distinct quantities; subtracting elapsed recovery seconds from an arrival-delay variable is not automatically valid. An episode continuing across a gate retains its latent state rather than triggering a new full delay. New future episodes remain separate opportunities.119120### C3. Boundary behavior121122Future/full-leg and current/remaining-leg distributions share one leg-level generative prior and latent performance state. With zero progress and no new information, conditioning is the identity operation: the distributions must agree in mixture probability, mean, variance and selected quantiles. A conditional-update implementation must pass this test before release; two independently fitted regressions with unmatched boundary distributions fail it.123124Run a second test that introduces measured entry speed, a recovery episode or wind change and confirms the forecast responds. Genuine new information may change variance and win probability at a gate. Do not impose the review's unconditional variance equality after informative observations, and do not smooth output curves to hide either legitimate changes or a broken model.125126## D. Reliability shape and observable tournament decisions127128Let q be an audited pooled full-race exogenous-failure probability draw and lambda=-log(1-q). With course fraction u, define nonnegative shape g(u), G=integral_0^1 g(v)dv, and hazard `h(u)=lambda*g(u)/G`. Survival from u to v is `exp(-integral_u^v h(w)dw)`. This preserves total risk q for every shape and conditions naturally on survival.129130Reference g=1. Sensitivity g=2 in the first leg or within the last 5% of a leg before a gate, and g=1 elsewhere, without double multiplying overlapping zones. These are explicitly hypothetical phase weights; six unreviewed terminal statuses do not fit them. Keep pre-gun DNS separate, and separate timerule failure from random reliability. A further hazard multiplier k uses `q_k=1-(1-q)^k`, never unbounded probability doubling.131132For AC75 match sensitivity, use `p_ij=logistic(c*(theta_i-theta_j))` with c in {1,2,3}. c changes match outcome noise conditional on ability; sigma changes persistent differences. These are not interchangeable across a mixed fleet/match tournament. No current evidence supports a particular real-world 2–3x ratio. A dominant-sweep fixture should react in the expected direction under c, without describing that response as empirical accuracy.133134Each simulated continuation contains its own public round-robin record. At the semifinal choice, select the eligible opponent with the lowest observed Challenger round-robin win fraction, excluding Defender results where required. Policy ties use pre-tournament predicted match strength and stable ID. Actual official selections override the policy. Its decision uses neither hidden persistent effects nor actual future results. A Bayesian policy would need a Bradley–Terry/ranking likelihood update from these observations; binary results do not have an automatic normal-normal conjugate posterior.135136## E. Rank convention177178In an eight-boat race with five finishers and three nonfinishers, tied-N scores sum to `1+2+3+4+5+8+8+8=39`. Mid-ranks would sum to 36. Both are coherent: they answer different questions. The chosen output is the expected tied-N score, so no conservation assertion belongs in its test. Preserve the agreed definition and label it rather than changing the target covertly.179180### F2. Win chance and mean rank can disagree181182Consider one joint distribution over three complete orders:183184| Order | Probability |185| --- | ---: |186| A, B, C | 0.45 |187| B, C, A | 0.40 |188| C, B, A | 0.15 |189190A has the highest win chance (0.45), but expected position 2.10; B has win chance 0.40 and expected position 1.60. All ranks sum to six. A coherent model therefore must not be tested by requiring the two orderings to agree.191192### F3. Small-sample release policy193194The revised P3 keeps three development dates and the descriptive audit, scores races chronologically and reports race-/day-equal summaries. It does not pretend a bootstrap repairs the independent sample size. Practical frozen guardrail budgets replace an exact-zero veto; a passing change is a provisional operational selection, not a significance or noninferiority finding. Live-only conditioning changes preserve pre-race distributions exactly; shared-strength/dynamics changes face pre-race checks too. A new model cannot omit its difficult early folds: its reference fallback is scored as part of the deployment procedure.195196The larger scientific requirement is more independent competitive data and a prospective frozen evaluation. No adjustment of three-day thresholds can substitute for that evidence.197198## Implementation status of this revision199200This review originally changed the specifications only.
